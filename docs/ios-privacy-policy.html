TrashVPN iOS 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

本政策适用于 TrashVPN iOS 应用，以及在兼容的 macOS 设备上运行的同一应用版本（以下统称“应用”）。本政策说明应用在注册 WARP 身份、筛选节点、建立 VPN 连接和提供相关功能时，如何处理信息。

1. 核心说明

TrashVPN 是独立开发的第三方客户端，使用 Cloudflare WARP 网络服务提供连接能力。应用不要求你向 TrashVPN 创建用户名、邮箱或密码账户。

当前版本不包含向开发者自有服务器上传浏览记录、广告标识符、分析事件或崩溃报告的功能。节点缓存、测速结果、应用设置和诊断日志主要保存在你的设备上。应用仍需要与 Cloudflare 及测速所用的第三方网络服务通信，才能完成注册、节点检测和 VPN 连接。

2. 应用会处理哪些信息

2.1 WARP 注册信息

首次准备某种连接协议时，应用会在设备上生成加密密钥，并向 Cloudflare WARP 注册接口提交相应的公钥和协议注册请求。Cloudflare 会收到该请求的 IP 地址、请求时间、网络连接信息及必要的 HTTP 技术请求头，并可能返回注册标识、访问令牌、隧道地址、对端公钥和可用端点。

私钥由应用在本机生成，不会作为注册公钥提交给 Cloudflare。注册标识、访问令牌以及密钥材料保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，用于后续连接。不同协议可能需要不同的注册字段；例如 MASQUE 注册请求会包含协议所需的设备类型、系统版本、语言和注册时间等技术信息，其中随机标识由应用生成，并非读取设备硬件序列号。

2.2 节点发现与连接质量测试

应用会探测 Cloudflare WARP 的候选网络端点，并通过 Cloudflare 的连通性测试和测速服务检查节点是否可用。启用下载测速时，应用会从 Cloudflare Speed Test 服务下载有限大小的测试数据，以计算连接速度。节点质量检查还可能向 YouTube 的连通性测试地址发送不含账户内容的测试请求。

Cloudflare、YouTube 及相关网络服务提供商会按照各自的服务机制接收请求 IP 地址、请求时间和必要的连接元数据。节点地址、延迟、稳定性和下载速度等测量结果保存在本机，用于节点排序、缓存和后续连接。

2.3 当前连接出口信息

VPN 连接成功后，应用可向 Cloudflare 查询当前 WARP 出口的国家代码和数据中心代码，并在界面中显示。这是基于网络响应得到的出口信息，不是设备 GPS 定位。应用不会为此请求精确地理位置权限。

2.4 VPN 流量与 DNS

启用 VPN 后，系统会按照你选择的节点和网络设置，将相应网络流量通过 Cloudflare WARP 网络转发。Cloudflare 及你访问的网站、应用和其他网络服务会在提供连接或内容时处理必要的网络流量和连接数据。

应用本身不建立开发者运营的 VPN 出口，也不会把你的浏览历史发送到开发者自有服务器。此说明不代表 Cloudflare、DNS 服务商、目的地网站或其他网络中间方不处理或不保留网络数据；这些第三方的数据处理受其自身政策约束。

DNS 默认使用 Cloudflare DNS（1.1.1.1），你也可以在设置中指定其他 DNS 服务。使用自定义 DNS 时，DNS 请求会由你选择的服务商处理；DNS 行为还会受到你在应用中选择的 DNS 设置影响。

3. 保存在设备上的信息

应用会在本机保存运行所需的数据，包括：

  • WARP 注册身份、访问令牌和密钥材料（保存在系统钥匙串中）；
  • 应用设置、所选协议、地址族、DNS 设置和节点选择偏好；
  • 候选端点、已完成的节点缓存、延迟与下载测速结果，以及可恢复的优选进度；
  • 连接状态、错误信息和用于排查问题的本地诊断日志。

应用使用 iOS 应用容器、应用组容器和系统钥匙串保存这些数据。部分文件使用 iOS 文件保护机制。应用不会因为这些数据保存在本机，就自动将它们上传给开发者。

4. 诊断日志与节点导出

应用提供连接诊断功能。诊断报告在本机生成，可能包含应用版本、协议、节点端点、测速摘要、连接状态、设置摘要和错误日志。报告不会自动上传；只有在你主动使用系统分享功能发送报告时，接收方才会获得你选择分享的内容。分享前请先检查报告，避免发送你不希望披露的网络或设备信息。

应用也允许你主动复制节点配置。导出的配置可能包含 VPN 私钥、对端公钥、隧道地址和节点端点。复制操作会将内容放入系统剪贴板；请只将配置粘贴到你信任的应用或设备中，不要公开分享。

5. 第三方服务与信息共享

为实现应用功能，应用会直接使用或连接以下第三方服务：

  • Cloudflare：WARP 身份注册、节点连接、连通性检查、测速和出口信息查询。
  • YouTube：在节点质量筛选过程中进行连通性测试。
  • DNS 服务商：由默认 DNS 或你自行配置的 DNS 服务商处理 DNS 请求。
  • Apple：由 iOS/macOS 系统提供 VPN 配置、Network Extension、钥匙串和应用分发等平台能力。

应用不出售个人信息，不在当前版本中展示应用内广告，也不集成开发者自有的广告追踪或使用分析服务。你主动打开应用内的外部链接或分享诊断报告时，相关信息将由你选择的第三方接收方按照其政策处理。

第三方政策：Cloudflare Privacy Policy；Google Privacy Policy。

6. 保存期限与删除

本机设置、节点缓存和诊断文件会在应用运行所需期间保留，直到被应用更新替换或由你删除。卸载应用通常会移除应用容器中的文件，但系统钥匙串项目可能在卸载后继续保留，具体取决于 iOS 的系统行为。

Cloudflare 和其他第三方对其收到的信息如何保存、使用或删除，由相应服务商决定。TrashVPN 不控制第三方的保存期限或删除流程。

7. 安全

应用通过加密网络连接与 Cloudflare 通信，并将注册身份和密钥材料保存在系统钥匙串中。我们会在应用实现范围内采取合理措施保护本机数据，但互联网传输、设备存储和 VPN 服务均无法保证绝对安全。请妥善保护设备锁屏凭据，不要向他人提供导出的私钥或诊断文件。

8. 儿童隐私

应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。我们不会有意通过应用向儿童收集个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

9. 跨境处理

Cloudflare、DNS 服务商、YouTube 及你访问的网络服务可能在不同国家或地区运营基础设施。使用应用时，相关网络请求可能由你所在地区以外的服务器处理。

10. 你的选择与隐私请求

你可以停止使用应用、关闭 VPN、修改 DNS 设置、停止节点测速，或不使用节点导出和诊断分享功能。对于由开发者实际控制且适用法律赋予你访问、更正或删除权利的信息，你可以通过下方联系方式提出请求。对于 Cloudflare 或其他第三方直接处理的信息，应向相应服务商提出请求。

11. 政策变更

当应用的数据处理方式或适用要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并标注新的生效日期。

12. 联系方式

如有隐私问题或请求，请发送邮件至 feedback@itmanager.top。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the TrashVPN iOS app and the same app when run on compatible macOS devices. It explains how information is handled when the app registers a WARP identity, discovers and tests nodes, establishes a VPN connection, and provides related features.

1. At a Glance

TrashVPN is an independently developed third-party client that uses the Cloudflare WARP network service. You do not need to create a username, email, or password account with the app developer.

The current app version does not implement a developer-operated service for uploading browsing history, advertising identifiers, analytics events, or crash reports. Node caches, measurements, settings, and diagnostic logs are primarily stored on your device. The app does communicate with Cloudflare and certain third-party network services to register, test nodes, and provide VPN connectivity.

2. Information Processed by the App

2.1 WARP Registration

When a protocol is prepared for the first time, the app generates cryptographic keys on your device and submits the corresponding public key and registration request to the Cloudflare WARP registration API. Cloudflare receives the request IP address, request time, network information, and necessary HTTP technical headers. Cloudflare may return a registration identifier, access token, tunnel addresses, peer public key, and available endpoints.

The private key is generated locally and is not submitted as the registration public key. Registration identifiers, access tokens, and key material are stored in the iOS Keychain for subsequent connections. Certain protocols may require additional technical registration fields. For example, a MASQUE registration request includes protocol-related device type, operating-system version, locale, and registration-time information. Any serial-like value in that request is randomly generated by the app; it is not read from your device hardware serial number.

2.2 Node Discovery and Connection Tests

The app probes candidate Cloudflare WARP endpoints and uses Cloudflare connectivity and speed-test services to evaluate node availability. When download testing is enabled, the app downloads a limited amount of test data from Cloudflare Speed Test to estimate throughput. Node quality checks may also send a connectivity request to a YouTube test endpoint; this request does not contain your YouTube account content.

Cloudflare, YouTube, and relevant network providers receive the request IP address, request time, and necessary connection metadata as part of providing these services. Endpoint addresses, latency, stability, and download measurements are stored locally to rank nodes, maintain a cache, and support later connections.

2.3 VPN Exit Information

After a VPN connection is established, the app may query Cloudflare for the country code and data-center code of the current WARP exit and display that information. This is network-derived exit information, not GPS location. The app does not request precise device-location permission for this feature.

2.4 VPN Traffic and DNS

When the VPN is enabled, the operating system routes applicable network traffic through the selected endpoint and the Cloudflare WARP network. Cloudflare and the websites, apps, and other network services you access process the network traffic and connection data necessary to provide connectivity and content.

The app does not operate its own VPN exit network and does not send your browsing history to a developer-operated server. This does not mean Cloudflare, DNS providers, destination services, or other network intermediaries do not process or retain network data. Their handling is governed by their own policies.

Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) is the default resolver. You may configure another DNS provider in the app. If you use a custom resolver, DNS requests are handled by that provider; actual DNS behavior also depends on your selected app settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores information needed to operate, which may include:

  • WARP registration identities, access tokens, and cryptographic key material in the system Keychain;
  • App settings, selected protocol, address family, DNS settings, and node-selection preferences;
  • Candidate endpoints, completed node caches, latency and download measurements, and resumable optimization progress;
  • Connection state, error details, and local diagnostic logs.

This information is stored in the app container, app-group container, and system Keychain. Some files use iOS file-protection features. Storage on your device does not cause the information to be automatically uploaded to the developer.

4. Diagnostics and Node Export

The app provides a connection-diagnostics feature. A report is generated locally and may contain the app version, protocol, endpoint, measurement summaries, connection state, settings summary, and error logs. It is not uploaded automatically. A recipient receives the report only if you choose to share it using the system share interface. Review the report before sharing it.

The app also lets you copy node configurations. An exported configuration may contain a VPN private key, peer public key, tunnel address, and endpoint. Copying places this information on the system clipboard. Only paste it into apps or devices you trust, and do not publish it.

5. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The app directly uses or connects to the following third-party services to provide its features:

  • Cloudflare: WARP registration, endpoint connectivity, speed testing, and VPN exit information.
  • YouTube: connectivity checks during node-quality screening.
  • DNS providers: DNS requests handled by the default or custom resolver you select.
  • Apple: operating-system capabilities such as VPN configuration, Network Extension, Keychain, and app distribution.

The app does not sell personal information, display in-app advertisements in the current version, or integrate a developer-operated advertising-tracking or usage-analytics service. If you open an external link or share a diagnostic report, the third-party recipient you choose handles the information under its own policy.

Third-party policies: Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local settings, node caches, and diagnostic files remain on your device while needed by the app, until replaced by the app or deleted by you. Uninstalling the app generally removes files in its app container, but Keychain items may remain after uninstall depending on iOS behavior.

Cloudflare and other third parties determine how they retain, use, or delete information they receive. TrashVPN does not control their retention periods or deletion procedures.

7. Security

The app communicates with Cloudflare over encrypted network connections and stores registration identities and key material in the system Keychain. We use reasonable measures within the app to protect local data, but internet transmission, device storage, and VPN services cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device passcode and do not disclose exported private keys or diagnostic files.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below.

9. International Processing

Cloudflare, DNS providers, YouTube, and the network services you access may operate infrastructure in different countries and regions. Network requests made while using the app may therefore be processed outside your country or region.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may stop using the app, disconnect the VPN, change DNS settings, disable node download testing, or choose not to export nodes or share diagnostics. Where applicable law gives you rights over information actually controlled by the developer, you may submit a request using the contact details below. Requests concerning information handled directly by Cloudflare or another third party should be directed to that provider.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy if the app's data practices or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email feedback@itmanager.top.
